# Receipt-aware completion and concise automation progress

A verified mail send was followed by a model completion rejection. External delivery receipts were also incorrectly classified as failed business evidence. Separate business receipts from delivery status. Finalize a run deterministically only when its last validated business assessment remains valid under current source authorization, every approved completion action has completed, and all required connector receipts retain matching fingerprints. Never replay completed writes or repair historical sends by rerunning them.

Automation's main workflow view should show concise, factual action outcomes. Preserve technical failures and full assessments in Details/Background, and keep actual required approvals and business-input requests actionable. Do not label a failed execution successful merely because mail was sent.
