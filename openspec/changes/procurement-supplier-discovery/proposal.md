# Supplier discovery and unified project observability
## Why
Procurement needs optional keyless supplier discovery and one communication view across its recorded child actions.
## What Changes
- Add bounded DDGS search and public-page reading tools.
- Persist evidence-backed candidates and require a reviewed shortlist before RFQs.
- Support supplied, discover and combined vendor modes without fixed resource IDs.
- Aggregate exact project run revisions/events into one namespaced communication graph.
## Impact
Native procurement only; preserve existing supplied-vendor workflows, contracts and approval gates. No live outreach during verification.
