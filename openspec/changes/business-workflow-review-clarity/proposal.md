# Business workflow review clarity

## Why
The owner cannot prepare a read-only investigation because its known coverage caveats were duplicated into unresolved setup requirements. The evidence panel exposes raw vocabulary IDs/hashes ahead of useful business meaning.

## What Changes
Conservatively separate explicit read-only coverage caveats from actionable setup requirements, retaining an audit and all limits. Preserve missing access, identity, input and write/approval requirements as blockers. Apply the correction through an immutable new draft revision for the owner's affected workflow. Present business definitions and workflow effect first; put source quotations and technical provenance behind optional disclosures.

## Impact
Planner requirement normalization, review UI and the affected draft. No fabricated relationships, missing-data concealment, automatic approval/publication/activation, model calls or company record writes.
